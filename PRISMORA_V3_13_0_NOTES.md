# Prismora 3.13.0

## Equalizer / DSP

- Equalizer added to the bottom navigation.
- Real-time PCM DSP for 16-bit, packed 24-bit, signed 32-bit and float PCM.
- Up to 24 filters with Peak, Low shelf, High shelf, Low pass and High pass types.
- Frequency, gain and Q editing with a live response graph.
- Preamp, compressor, limiter and crossfeed.
- Flat, Bass boost, Vocal clarity, Warm, Bright and V-shape presets.
- Equalizer APO / AutoEQ text import and export.
- Saved automatic DSP profiles per output device.
- Enabling DSP disables the native bit-perfect route and reports `DSP ACTIVE`.

## Player / visualizer

- Player action layout simplified to Lyrics left and Repeat right.
- Duplicate Lyrics control, Player Playback button and Player Shuffle button removed.
- Queue remains available by swiping up; Shuffle and Sleep remain in playback settings/notification where applicable.
- Bars, Line, Radial and Minimal visualizer styles.
- Spectrum analysis reduced to a reusable 1024-point FFT path without requiring Lite Mode.

## Themes

- Prismora Glass and Prismora OLED.
- Existing Miku, Teto, Neru, Yi Xi, KAITO, MEIKO, Inabakumori, Cover Wave/Backdrop and P3R/P4G/P5R retained.
- Insomnia, Cyberpunk, CRT, Y2K, Vaporwave, Cassette, MiniDisc and Portable Player added.
- Static Miku peek remains removed.

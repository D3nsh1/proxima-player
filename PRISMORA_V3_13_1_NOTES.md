# Prismora 3.13.1

- Cassette theme now has a dedicated blue personal-stereo seek deck based on the supplied layout reference.
- The standard seek slider is hidden only for the Cassette theme.
- Album art, track/artist, elapsed/total time, cassette reels and tape progress are integrated into the deck.
- Hold `HOLD ≫` for 350 ms to start a 10× fast-forward preview; release to commit one seek operation.
- A short tap does not seek, preventing accidental position changes.
- Cyberpunk primary color changed from cyan to yellow. Cyan is retained as its secondary accent.
